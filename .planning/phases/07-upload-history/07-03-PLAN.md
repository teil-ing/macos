Build and run the app, then verify each scenario:

**1. Empty state**
- Open the menu bar popover
- Verify the history section shows a clock icon and "No uploads yet" text
- Verify the section header says "Recent Uploads (0)"
- Verify popover width looks wider than before (~320pt)

**2. Upload creates history entry**
- Take a screenshot (any capture mode)
- Wait for upload to succeed (checkmark + sound)
- Open the popover
- Verify a new entry appears at the top with:
  - A small rounded thumbnail (recognizable as the captured image)
  - A relative timestamp like "just now" or "0 seconds ago"
  - A copy icon (doc.on.doc) button
  - An open-in-browser (arrow.up.right.square) button
- Verify the section header now says "Recent Uploads (1)"

**3. Copy URL action**
- Click the copy icon on a history entry
- Verify the icon changes to a green checkmark
- Wait ~1.5 seconds — verify it reverts back to the copy icon
- Paste somewhere — verify the teil.ing share URL was copied

**4. Open in browser action**
- Click the open-in-browser icon on a history entry
- Verify the share URL opens in the default browser

**5. Multiple entries**
- Take 2-3 more screenshots
- Verify entries stack newest-first
- Verify each has its own distinct thumbnail

**6. Persistence across restart**
- Quit the app (Cmd+Q or Quit button)
- Relaunch the app
- Open the popover
- Verify all history entries from before the restart are still visible
- Verify thumbnails still load correctly

**7. Delete entry (swipe or context menu)**
- Right-click a history entry and select "Delete"
- Verify the entry disappears from the list
- Verify the count in the header decreases

**8. Clear All**
- Click the "Clear" button in the section header
- Verify a confirmation dialog appears: "Clear all upload history?"
- Click "Clear All"
- Verify all entries are removed and the empty state returns
- Verify the section header says "Recent Uploads (0)"

**9. Timestamp auto-refresh (optional)**
- Take a screenshot, note the timestamp shows "just now" or similar
- Wait 1-2 minutes
- Open the popover — verify the timestamp updated to "1 minute ago" or "2 minutes ago"
